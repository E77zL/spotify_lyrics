Spotify Lyrics HUD Companion 2.0.3

Derived from Fanbeixi/spotify-lyrics-HUD_CN, HUD release 5.1.0 (MIT).
https://github.com/Fanbeixi/spotify-lyrics-HUD_CN
The upstream window, settings, assets, LRC utilities, SMTC worker and QQ provider
foundation are used and adapted. This is a derivative integration, not the
original author's release. Original MIT text is preserved in licenses/HUD-LICENSE.txt.

Integration changes: Spotify lifecycle attachment through an in-process native module,
authenticated loopback controls and playback-clock bridge, A button, quick fades,
stable lyric zoom, NetEase fallback, strict track matching, local-file handling,
per-song timing, request cancellation, bounded caching, coalesced settings writes,
and automatic fullscreen click-through via a small Windows foreground observer.
Source is maintained once under app/, extension/, native/ and installer/.
